# Bundled audio decoder notices

Rustic bundles unmodified Symphonia 0.6.1 libraries under the Mozilla Public
License 2.0. Their source is available from
[Symphonia](https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia) and the versioned
[crates.io packages](https://crates.io/crates/symphonia/0.6.1).
The bundled packages are `symphonia`, `symphonia-core`, `symphonia-common`,
`symphonia-metadata`, `symphonia-bundle-mp3`, `symphonia-bundle-flac`,
`symphonia-codec-aac`, and `symphonia-format-isomp4`, all version 0.6.1.
Their license is included in `Symphonia-MPL-2.0.txt`.

Rustic also bundles [ogg 0.8.0](https://crates.io/crates/ogg/0.8.0) and
[opus-pure 0.2.2](https://crates.io/crates/opus-pure/0.2.2) under the
BSD 3-Clause license. See `ogg-BSD-3-Clause.txt`,
`opus-pure-BSD-3-Clause.txt`, and `opus-pure-ATTRIBUTION.md` for their notices.
