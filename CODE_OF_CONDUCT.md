# Rustic Engine Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Rustic Engine to be a welcoming community where people can learn, collaborate, and build together, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to treat others with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give feedback about ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept constructive criticism and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect others’ privacy, time, and boundaries.
- Help newcomers participate and learn.

Disagreement is welcome. Personal hostility is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory comments.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouragement of violence.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sharing someone’s private information without permission.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, community channels, and project events. It also applies when someone officially represents Rustic Engine in public.

## Reporting Concerns

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer using a private contact method listed in the repository or on their profile. If that maintainer is involved in the concern, contact another maintainer.

Include relevant context, links, or screenshots when possible. You do not need to confront the person involved before making a report.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share information only as needed to assess and address the concern. Complete confidentiality cannot always be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review concerns fairly, consider the context and impact, and avoid participating in decisions where they have a conflict of interest.

Depending on the severity or repetition of the behavior, actions may include:

- A request to stop or correct the behavior.
- A private or public warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- A permanent ban from project spaces.

Serious violations may result in immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

## Accountability

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone, including maintainers. Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through a private maintainer contact, preferably with a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.
