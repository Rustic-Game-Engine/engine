## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately through
[GitHub's vulnerability reporting page](https://github.com/Rustic-Game-Engine/engine/security/advisories/new).

Do not disclose vulnerability details in public issues, discussions, or
pull requests before maintainers have investigated and coordinated disclosure.

Please include:

- The affected version or commit.
- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce it or a minimal proof of concept.
- Relevant platform details, configuration, and logs.

Remove credentials, personal information, and other sensitive data from
your report.

## Response Process

We aim to acknowledge reports within seven days and provide progress
updates at least every fourteen days while investigating. These are
targets, not guaranteed response times.

If a vulnerability is confirmed, we will work toward a fix and coordinate
disclosure with you. Timing depends on severity and complexity.

If a report is declined or cannot be reproduced, we will explain our
reasoning and may request additional information.

We will credit reporters with their permission. You may also choose to
remain anonymous.
